Fusion branch

A transparent decision-level pool

wm ∝validation reliability×(0.5 + 0.5 · confidencem)

Confidence is one minus normalized entropy. Available weights are re-normalized when a modality is missing; the fused vector is the weighted linear opinion pool. Agreement uses one minus pairwise total-variation distance.

Interpretation guardrails

Confidence is not certainty.

  • High confidence can still be wrong under distribution shift.
  • Grad-CAM indicates model attention, not clinical causality.
  • Local numerical influence is one-feature-at-a-time sensitivity against training medians.
  • Conflicting modalities are surfaced, never hidden.
