Dataset & methodology

Designed around what the data proves.

Scientific constraints drive the architecture—not the other way around.

Critical design decision

Independent training. Session-level fusion.

The facial images, speech recordings, and numerical profiles have no shared participant or sample identifier and contain different sample counts. MindFuse never zips or randomly pairs them. Each branch learns only from its own labels; probability vectors are fused only for inputs supplied together in a real assessment session.

A

Facial emotion

48×48 grayscale expressions across Angry, Disgust, Fear, Happy, Sad, Surprise, and Neutral.

  • Compact residual CNN trained and evaluated from scratch
  • Predeclared HOG small-data primary when audit finds <1,000 images
  • Class-balanced logistic and RBF-SVM candidates selected on validation macro-F1
  • Decision-preserving temperature calibration
  • Model-tied occlusion sensitivity or Grad-CAM
B

Speech emotion

RAVDESS-style WAV clips across eight emotions and 24 actors.

  • 16 kHz mono, peak normalization
  • 4-second, 64-bin log-Mel representation
  • Actor-disjoint train/validation/test
  • Residual spectrogram CNN from scratch
  • Input-gradient audio saliency
C

Numerical indicators

18 behavioral, facial, acoustic, and physiological predictors with four stress labels and three score targets.

  • Stratified 70/15/15 row split
  • Preprocessing fit within sklearn Pipelines
  • Validation-based model comparison
  • Calibrated four-class probabilities
  • Multi-output D/A/S regression

Scope & limitations

What this system does not claim.

Not a diagnostic device. Labels and scores are dataset-defined model estimates, not clinical diagnoses.

No longitudinal context. One session cannot establish a psychiatric condition or trajectory.

Potential bias and shift. Actor demographics, facial capture conditions, language, devices, and questionnaire construction constrain generalization.

Fusion is decision-level. Without paired data, cross-modal interaction learning would be scientifically unsupported.
